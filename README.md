A comprehensive, bilingual (English & Telugu), mobile-first field guidance portal designed for **Election Officers, Returning Officers (RO/ARO), Presiding Officers (PO/APO), Zonal Officers, and Route Officers** on duty for the Phase 2 Ordinary Elections to **Srikalahasti Municipality, Tirupati District, Andhra Pradesh**.

---

## 🌐 Live Access
- **Public URL**: [https://bapanayya.github.io/Election-Duty-2026/](https://bapanayya.github.io/Election-Duty-2026/)
- **Offline Capable**: The application is packaged in a self-contained single-file architecture (`index.html`) that works completely offline on smartphones, tablets, and laptops without internet connectivity.

---

## 📋 Key Modules & Features

### 1. 📅 Election Schedule & Statutory Time Limits
- Full chronological timeline from SEC Notification (12.11.2026) through Nominations (16.11 to 18.11), Scrutiny (19.11), Appeals, Withdrawals (22.11), Poll Day (29.11.2026, 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM), to Counting of Votes (03.12.2026).
- Statutory rule-by-rule time limits table under AP Conduct of Election Rules, 2026.

### 2. 🚶 9 Operational Stages
- Step-by-step operational workflows and legal provisions for all 9 phases:
  1. Election Notice & Ward Reservation Publishing
  2. Nomination Receipt & Security Deposit Management
  3. Scrutiny of Nominations
  4. Appeals & Final List of Valid Nominations
  5. Withdrawal of Candidature & Symbol Allotment
  6. Preparation for Poll & Polling Staff Dispatch
  7. Conduct of Poll (Poll Day Procedures & Ballot Box Sealing)
  8. Reception & Safe Custody of Polled Boxes
  9. Counting of Votes & Declaration of Results

### 3. ✅ 8 Interactive Checklists (40 Items)
- Touch-friendly checkboxes (≥ 48px targets) across 8 election stages.
- Real-time progress bar and persistent tick state saved in `localStorage`.
- Reset and Print options.

### 4. 📌 Important Guidelines
- Simple, clear guidance points covering Dual Simultaneous Elections (Direct Chairperson + 44 Ward Members), Ballot Paper Colors (White Wove for Chairperson, Pink Wove for Ward Members), Tendered/Challenged votes, and four-color envelope protocol at poll close.

### 5. ⚖️ Do's & Don'ts Matrix
- Comprehensive comparison matrix covering Model Code of Conduct (MCC), Nomination handling, Scrutiny, Polling day, and Counting halls.

### 6. 📂 18 Clean Statutory & Non-Statutory Forms & Proformas
Extracted and cleaned from the official election authority document **`Election-Officers-Forms.pdf`** with all handwritten marks removed:
- **Form - I**: Nomination Paper [Rule 8(1)] with 10 candidate/proposer fields, MCC declaration, and RO acceptance/rejection order.
- **Form - II**: Declaration by SC / ST / BC Candidate [Rule 8(8)] with Deputy Tahsildar verification.
- **Form - I A**: Choice of Symbols Declaration [Rule 8(9)].
- **Statutory Deposit Register**: 14-column register for security deposits (₹3,000 / ₹1,500).
- **Nomination Scrutiny Checklist**: 6-point verification with missing documents notice.
- **Self-Declaration Affidavit (SEC Order No. 191/2026)**: Complete 7-page affidavit covering PAN/ITR, court cases, movable & immovable assets, govt dues, and 2 witnesses.
- **Declaration of No Disqualification**: Under AP Municipalities Act, 1965 (Sec 13-16).
- **Expenditure Limits Declaration**: Under SEC Order No. 197 & Notification No. 177/2026 (₹20L Chairperson / ₹7.5L Ward Member).
- **Proforma - I**: Expenditure Register Cover Page.
- **Proforma - II**: Daily Expense Index (6 columns).
- **Proforma - III**: Daily Itemized Account Sheet (7 columns) with candidate affirmation.
- **Proforma - IV**: Statutory Acknowledgment Receipt for Accounts Submission.
- **Form - 1**: Notice of Election [Rule 4].
- **Form - VII**: List of Contesting Candidates [Rule 14(1)].
- **Form - X-C**: Appointment of Polling Agent [Rule 18(1)].
- **Form - XVIII**: Ballot Paper Account (Part-I) [Rule 38].
- **Form - XX (Part-I)**: Result of Counting Sheet [Rule 48(1)] with mandatory 15-minute recount window.
- **Form - XX (Part-II)**: Declaration of Result & Return [Rule 51].

### 7. 🏛️ Srikalahasti Field Roster & Directory
- **44 Wards Master Chart**: Complete reservation categorization (ST: 1, SC: 4, BC: 17, UR: 22 — exactly 22 Women seats / 50%).
- **88 Polling Stations**: Mobile Card View with instant search, ward filtering, and one-touch **Google Maps Navigation (`📍 Map Location`)**.
- **67 Direct Tap-to-Call Buttons**: Green touch pills (`📞`) for Cluster Offices (12), Zonal Officers (3), Route Officers (9), and MCC/FST/SST Surveillance Teams.

### 8. 🛠️ Field Tools & Calculators
- Ballot Paper Requirement Calculator (Ward + 10% reserve).
- 5:00 PM Queue Slip Token Generator.
- 4 Color-Coded Envelope Packing Guide (Brown, Green, Yellow, Blue).
- Doubtful / Invalid Vote Decision Principles.
